Mission Statement

About DCHA & Open Access Mission

Democratizing cultural knowledge across India's 28 states and 8 union territories.

Preserving India's Cultural Legacy

The Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA) is an academic cultural technology initiative built to systematically document, preserve, and showcase the tangible and intangible heritage of India.

Open Licensing & Media Sources

All photographs, archival texts, and historical records on DCHA are aggregated under Creative Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0), Public Domain, or official government open-data archives (ASI, UNESCO, Ministry of Culture).
