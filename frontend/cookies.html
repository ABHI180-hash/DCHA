Cookie Policy

How the Digital Culture Heritage Archive utilizes essential browser storage, local preferences, and privacy controls.

Last Updated: October 30, 2026  |  Platform Principle: Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers  |  User Control: Customizable via Consent Manager

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and browser Web Storage (specifically localStorage and sessionStorage) are small text and data files stored on your device by your web browser. They allow web applications to remember your logged-in session, interface preferences, and locally saved cultural bookmarks without requiring continuous server re-authentication.

2. Our Storage Philosophy: No Commercial Tracking

Unlike commercial platforms, the Digital Culture Heritage Archive does not use third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site profiling scripts. We believe cultural heritage research should be conducted in private.

We use browser storage strictly for system security, persistent authentication, user interface preferences (such as light or dark theme), and user-curated favorites.

3. Complete Inventory of Storage Keys Used

Below is an exhaustive inventory of the client-side storage keys utilized by the DCHA application:

Category Storage Key Type & Lifespan Purpose
Strictly Necessary dcha_token LocalStorage / 24 hours Contains the cryptographic JWT token required to authenticate authorized API requests.
Strictly Necessary dcha_user LocalStorage / Session Caches the authenticated user's display name, role, and avatar for immediate navigation display.
Strictly Necessary dcha_cookie_consent LocalStorage / 12 months Records your explicit cookie consent preferences so you are not repeatedly prompted.
Functional Preferences dcha_theme LocalStorage / Persistent Stores your UI mode preference (light parchment vs. obsidian dark mode).
Functional Preferences dcha_favorites LocalStorage / Persistent Maintains your personal list of saved heritage site IDs locally on your browser.
Functional Preferences dcha_collections LocalStorage / Persistent Allows offline creation and editing of customized archival study anthologies.
Optional Analytics (None active by default) Optional / Opt-in Reserved for privacy-preserving, self-hosted aggregate performance diagnostics. No data is sent to advertising networks.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

In addition to our on-site Cookie Consent Manager, you can manage or delete cookies directly through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Note: Disabling strictly necessary storage (such as dcha_token) will prevent you from signing in to your member account or submitting new contributions.
