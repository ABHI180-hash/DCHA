Institutional Information & Contact

Official contact desks, editorial leadership, and statutory grievance mechanisms for the Digital Culture Heritage Archive.

Operating Entity: Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA) Initiative  |  Mission: Academic Cultural Technology & Digital Humanities  |  Jurisdiction: Republic of India

1. Institutional Identity & Ownership

The Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA) is an independent academic, cultural technology, and digital humanities initiative dedicated to digitizing, categorizing, and democratizing access to India's monumental, living, culinary, and musical heritage.

The project operates in collaboration with researchers, cultural historians, and open-knowledge advocates, leveraging open public records from the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI), UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Ministry of Culture, and state cultural directorates.

2. Dedicated Institutional Desks

To ensure your inquiries are handled promptly by the appropriate department, please address communications to the specific desks below:

🏛️ Curatorial & Archival Desk

For historical inquiries, corrections to monument dossiers, cultural submissions, or scholar collaborations.

Email: curator@dcha.org

⚖️ Privacy & Grievance Desk

For statutory inquiries under the DPDP Act 2023, data access/erasure requests, and privacy concerns.

Email: privacy@dcha.org

📸 Media Licensing & Attribution

For inquiries regarding Creative Commons licenses, photography credits, or copyright notices.

Email: licensing@dcha.org

♿ Accessibility & Tech Desk

For reporting digital accessibility barriers, assistive tech issues, or technical vulnerabilities.

Email: accessibility@dcha.org

3. Institutional Details to be Supplied by Deployment Custodian

📌 Note for Deployment Custodian / Organization Owner:
Under Rule 3(2) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021, once deployed by a formal legal entity or university department, the following verified organizational credentials should be added to this section:
  • Registered Institutional Name & Non-Profit / University Affiliation
  • Physical Registered Office Address
  • Official Landline / Institutional Helpline Telephone Number
  • Physical Office Hours for Statutory Notice Service

4. Grievance Redressal Timeline

In adherence to the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the DPDP Act 2023:

  • Acknowledgment: Every formal grievance received at privacy@dcha.org is acknowledged within 48 hours with a unique tracking reference.
  • Investigation: The curatorial and technical audit team investigates the matter within 15 days.
  • Resolution: A formal written response or remedial action report is provided within a statutory maximum of 30 days.
