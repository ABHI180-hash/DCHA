Privacy Policy

How the Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA) collects, safeguards, and processes personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Effective Date: October 30, 2026  |  Statutory Framework: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) & Information Technology Act, 2000 (India)  |  Status: Active Archival Policy

1. Introduction & Institutional Scope

The Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA) is an academic and cultural technology initiative established to document, digitize, and safeguard the tangible and intangible heritage of India across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories.

This Privacy Policy explains how DCHA (referred to as "we", "our", or "the Archive") functions as a Data Fiduciary under Section 2(i) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We are committed to transparency, minimal data collection, non-commercial use, and empowering individuals with full control over their personal data.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We adhere strictly to the principle of Data Minimization. We collect only information essential to operate archival membership, maintain user collections, and review public cultural submissions:

Category Data Elements Purpose & Legal Basis
Account Credentials Full Name, Email Address, Bcrypt-Hashed Password User authentication, account recovery, session security (DPDP Section 4: Specified Purpose with Consent).
Member Profile Primary Cultural Role (Researcher, Enthusiast, Contributor), Optional Bio, Avatar Selection Personalizing the archival interface, community attribution of contributions.
Archival Interactions Saved Heritage Monuments, Custom Archival Collections, Submission History Delivering member-curated collections and research folders across sessions.
Community Submissions Heritage topic, description, cultural significance, media URLs, provenance citations Scholarly peer review and public educational publication under Creative Commons licensing.
Technical Metadata Browser user-agent, standard HTTP request headers, IP address (transient server logs) System security, DDoS defense, and ensuring API rate-limit integrity (Section 7: Legitimate Uses).

Sensitive Data Notice: DCHA does not collect Aadhaar numbers, biometric records, political affiliations, physical health information, or financial/banking details.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

In accordance with Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data is processed solely under:

  • Informed, Free, and Unambiguous Consent: Provided explicitly during registration and contribution submission via un-checked opt-in checkboxes.
  • Specified Purpose: Data provided for cultural collections or newsletter updates is never used for automated profiling or commercial exploitation.
  • Legitimate Uses: Security monitoring, fraud detection, and maintaining server integrity under Section 7 of the Act.

4. Storage, Security & Encryption

We implement robust technical and organizational security safeguards:

  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed using salted BCrypt algorithms before persistence; plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • Stateless Authentication: User sessions employ cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with a 256-bit secret key and time-limited expiration.
  • Transport Encryption: All communications between the client and server must occur over encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access Restrictions: Administrative database access is restricted to authorized curators under role-based authorization (RBAC).

5. Third-Party Data Disclosures

DCHA is a non-commercial educational archive. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data to commercial data brokers or advertisers. Disclosures occur only in the following limited technical capacities:

  • Heritage AI Curatorial Engine: When interacting with the AI Assistant, user prompts are processed server-side. If external LLM APIs (such as Google Gemini) are activated, only the cultural inquiry text is transmitted; user identity, email, and private collection records are strictly stripped.
  • Media & Font CDNs: Open-access media assets from Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash, and web fonts from Google Fonts, are loaded via their respective content delivery networks. These services may log transient IP addresses in standard web server logs.
  • Legal Obligations: Information will be disclosed only when explicitly required by a court of competent jurisdiction or authorized law enforcement agency under applicable Indian law.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

  • Active Accounts: User account data is retained for the lifetime of the active account.
  • Inactive Data / Account Deletion: Users may delete their account at any time via the Profile → Data Rights panel or by emailing the Grievance Officer. Upon deletion, personal profile data, authentication credentials, and custom collections are permanently purged within 30 days.
  • Published Cultural Contributions: Submissions already published under Creative Commons licenses may be retained for archival continuity with contributor attribution anonymized upon request.

7. Data Principal Rights under DPDP Act 2023

Under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access Information (Section 11): Request a summary of personal data processed, identities of third parties with whom data was shared, and an export of your records.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Update inaccurate or outdated profile details directly via your profile page, or request complete erasure.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Seek prompt resolution for any privacy concerns through our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): Nominate an individual to exercise data rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time without retroactive impact on prior lawful processing.

8. Data Protection & Grievance Officer

In compliance with Section 13 of the DPDP Act 2023 and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the designated Grievance Officer for DCHA is:

Designation: Data Protection & Grievance Officer

9. Updates to this Policy

As Indian data protection regulations evolve and new rules under the DPDP Act 2023 are notified by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), this policy will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the Archive constitutes acknowledgment of updated terms.
