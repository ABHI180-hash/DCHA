Data Protection & User Rights

How you can exercise your statutory rights as a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Legal Reference: Chapter III, Sections 11–14, Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)  |  Designated Channel: privacy@dcha.org  |  In-App Controls: Member Profile → Data Rights Panel

1. Overview of Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023), you are recognized as a Data Principal. As the Data Fiduciary, DCHA is legally obligated to facilitate the seamless exercise of your rights regarding personal data collected during your archival interactions.

📥 1. Right to Access (Section 11)

You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data processed by DCHA, the specific purposes of processing, and details of any third parties involved in technical processing.

Export My Archival Data →

✏️ 2. Right to Correction (Section 12)

You have the right to correct inaccurate or misleading personal data, complete incomplete records, and update your profile bio, cultural role, and account credentials.

Update Profile Details →

🗑️ 3. Right to Erasure (Section 12)

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your personal account data once the specified purpose is fulfilled or upon withdrawal of consent.

Delete My Account →

⚖️ 4. Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

You have the right to an accessible, transparent mechanism for redressing any grievances regarding data handling, with a formal resolution within 30 days.

Submit Grievance →

2. How to Exercise Your Rights Directly in the Application

We have engineered self-service data management tools directly into the application so you can exercise your rights immediately without submitting bureaucratic paperwork:

  1. Immediate Data Export: Log in to your account and navigate to your Member Profile. Under the Data Rights & Privacy panel, click "Export My Archival Data" to download a complete, readable JSON file containing your registered name, email, role, bio, saved favorites, and submitted contributions.
  2. Direct Profile Correction: On the Profile page, click "Edit Profile" to update your display name or bio. Your changes take effect immediately across all archival interactions.
  3. One-Click Account Deletion: In your Profile panel, click "Delete My Account". After entering your confirmation, all authentication tokens, profile details, and custom collection folders are permanently expunged from the database.

3. Right to Nominate (Section 14)

Under Section 14 of the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to nominate any other individual who shall, in the event of your death or incapacity, exercise your data rights. To register a formal nomination for high-volume historical contributors, please send a written request to privacy@dcha.org including:

  • Your registered member email address.
  • Nominee's full legal name, relationship, and verified contact email.
  • Scope of nomination (e.g., stewardship of historical contributions or full account closure).

4. Formal Grievance Redressal Mechanism

If you have reason to believe your personal data has been handled inconsistently with the DPDP Act 2023, or if you encounter difficulties exercising your rights, you may submit a formal complaint directly to our Grievance Desk:

To: The Grievance Officer, Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA)
Official Email: privacy@dcha.org
Subject Line Format: [DPDP Grievance] - <Your Registered Email>
Statutory Acknowledgment: Within 48 hours.
Investigation & Resolution: Within 30 calendar days.
Escalation: If unresolved within 30 days, you have the statutory right to escalate your complaint to the Data Protection Board of India once established and notified under Section 18 of the DPDP Act 2023.
