2. Open Access Mission & Purpose

DCHA is an open-access cultural knowledge infrastructure created for educational, research, and cultural preservation purposes. Access to the archive, including its 36 States & UTs directory, 5,000-year historical timeline, audio recordings, virtual museum, and interactive maps, is provided free of charge to researchers, students, and citizens worldwide.

  • For certified historical claims, users are encouraged to cross-reference the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) and UNESCO World Heritage Center archives.
