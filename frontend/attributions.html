1. Media Governance & Intellectual Property Standards

The Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA) strictly respects copyright, artist moral rights, and open-knowledge standards. We never assume a cultural image is copyright-free simply because it appears online. All media indexed across our 36 state dossiers, virtual museum, and thematic exhibitions is drawn from verified open-access archives, academic repositories, or explicit public domain declarations.

2. Primary Institutional Partners & Open Repositories

  • Archaeological Survey of India (ASI): Public domain architectural blueprints, monument telemetry, and epigraphical transcripts.
  • UNESCO World Heritage Centre: World Heritage monument documentation, inscription criteria, and conservation state reports.
  • Wikimedia Commons: High-resolution architectural photography licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA).
  • Unsplash Open License: Freely usable cultural and landscape photography contributed by generous artists worldwide.
  • Ministry of Culture, Government of India: Open data feeds and festival schedules catalogued under the Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India.

4. Notice and Takedown Procedure

If you are a copyright owner or legal representative and believe that any media item hosted on DCHA has been incorrectly attributed, misidentified, or displayed without appropriate licensing permissions, please contact our Media Licensing Desk immediately:

Media Licensing Desk: Digital Culture Heritage Archive (DCHA)
Takedown & Licensing Email: licensing@dcha.org
Subject Line: [Media Notice] - <Asset Title or URL>
Procedure: Upon receipt of a valid notice, our team will temporarily remove or restrict access to the challenged asset within 24 hours while an expedited provenance review is conducted.
